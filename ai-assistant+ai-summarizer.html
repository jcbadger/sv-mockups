About AI Summarizer

The AI Summarizer generates a concise summary of the currently open document using ShareVault's AI service.

Summaries are generated from the document's indexed text. Redacted content will appear redacted in summaries even for users with unredacted access.

Summary content is subject to the same watermarking and copy protection policies as the document itself.

AI-generated content may contain errors. Always verify important information against the original document.

About ShareVault AI Assistant

ShareVault AI Assistant answers user questions about the documents in this ShareVault using a generative AI model (Claude 4.6 in this ShareVault).

Chat content is private — your questions and AI responses are never visible to other users or administrators. What we track

Redacted content will be excluded in responses even for users with permission to view redacted documents.

AI-generated responses may contain errors. Always verify important information against the original documents.

ShareVault logs usage metadata for governance purposes — including response times, document citations, and AI-generated topic labels — but never the content of your questions or responses.
